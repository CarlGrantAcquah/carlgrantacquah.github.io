Privacy policy

How Tema Home Care uses your details.

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Tema Home Care (run by Carl Grant, Tema, Ghana) connects customers in Tema, Ashaiman, Spintex, Sakumono and Community 25 with local technicians for AC servicing and gas refill, fumigation and pest control, and sofa, carpet and mattress cleaning. This page explains what we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your name and phone / WhatsApp number
  • Your area and the address for the job (when you book)
  • The service you need and its details (for example number of AC units, rooms, sofa seats), your preferred date and your property type
  • Photos or videos you choose to send us on WhatsApp
  • Payment records (amount, date, Job ID and reference). Card and Mobile Money details are handled by Paystack and are never seen or stored by us

We collect this when you fill in our website booking form, a Facebook or Instagram lead form, or when you message or call us.

Why we collect it

  • To contact you about your request on WhatsApp or by phone
  • To arrange a free estimate, give you a price and book the job
  • To share the job details (your name, area or address, phone number and the work needed) with the technician who will do the job
  • To record payments and handle complaints or refunds

We don't sell your details, and we don't use them for anything unrelated to your service. We'll only message you about your request or job, unless you ask us to keep in touch.

Where your data is kept

Booking requests are stored in a private Google Sheet (Google Forms and Google Sheets) that only Tema Home Care can access. Chats happen on WhatsApp. Payments are processed by Paystack. Each of these services has its own privacy policy. We keep job records for as long as we need them to provide the service and keep accurate accounts.

Advertising

Our website may use the Meta Pixel to measure whether our Facebook and Instagram ads lead to booking requests. Meta may use cookies for this. You can control ad settings in your Facebook or Instagram account.

Your choices: see, correct or delete your data

You can ask us at any time to show you, correct or delete the details we hold about you. Just send a WhatsApp message to +233 55 932 8385, and we'll do it and confirm on WhatsApp. (We may need to keep basic payment records for accounting.)
